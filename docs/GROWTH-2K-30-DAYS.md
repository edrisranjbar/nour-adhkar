# Nour: 2,000 combined store installs in 30 days

Prepared 27 October 2026. Acquisition window: **28 October–26 November 2026**, Asia/Tehran. Budget: **zero**, confirmed by the owner. Use existing app features and approved marketing assets.

## Decision

Make relevant community partnerships the main growth experiment. Use Instagram to demonstrate the app, referrals to extend distribution, and SEO to improve discovery and the path to installation. Do not spend this month building new features or publishing large volumes of generic articles.

The original planning scenario used the owner's approximate **1,000 Bazaar + 50 Myket = 1,050**, leaving **about 950**, or **31.67 new installs/day**. That historical estimate is not a currently measured combined baseline. Public store badges and our admin scraper are rounded; use private store reports for exact daily acquisition. A combined total is a sum of store counts, not a deduplicated count of people across stores. Do not count updates, reinstalls or website clicks as first-time installs.

Measurement setup was confirmed complete by the owner on 29 October, except Myket: the owner reports that its install figure is not visible until 100 installs. Keep Myket **unavailable / below the reporting threshold**; do not substitute zero, the earlier approximate 50, or website clicks. Keep the Bazaar metric's existing definition. When Myket becomes visible, record that first observed figure/date; it does not reconstruct the preceding daily history. Until then, the exact combined total and remaining daily requirement are unavailable. Bazaar-only totals may be shown as a clearly labelled confirmed lower bound, without claiming they equal the combined total.

## What the evidence says

- Search Console, Web, **28 Sep–25 Oct 2026**: **53 clicks / 604 impressions / 8.8% displayed CTR / average position 6.3**. Calculated CTR is 53/604 = 8.775%, consistent with the UI rounding. No country or device restriction was applied.
- Three-month context, **26 Jul–25 Oct**: **94 clicks / 1,172 impressions / 8.0% CTR / position 6.2**. This is context, not an equal-period comparison.
- Query examples in the 28-day view: «اذکار نور» 13/21 clicks/impressions; «اذکار صبحگاهی اهل سنت صوتی» 6/59; «اذکار شامگاهی اهل سنت صوتی» 3/17; «برنامه اذکار صبح و شام اهل سنت» 0/21. Query rows are not the whole traffic total; anonymized/omitted queries mean their sum need not reconcile.
- The older claim of 10 installs/day is a historical owner statement. Treat 300/month as a scenario until the private-panel baseline is checked.
- At current search scale, even 20% of 53 search clicks becoming installs would be only about 11 installs/28 days. That 20% is an illustrative assumption, not an observed conversion rate. SEO cannot responsibly be assigned the whole 950-install gap.

## Daily operating routine (about 90 minutes)

1. **10 minutes:** continue the existing tracking setup; enter yesterday's available Bazaar values and store-page conversion figures. Myket remains blank with status `unavailable_below_100` until its figure is displayed. Use Tehran dates and complete days. Never backfill unknown days with zero or spread the first visible Myket total across previous days.
2. **25 minutes:** contact two carefully selected page/channel/community admins, using a personal opening and a prepared demo. Record accepted/declined/pending and actual placement date. Follow up once after 3–4 days. Do not send unsolicited bulk messages.
3. **35 minutes:** create/publish the scheduled short demo or useful carousel; reuse real app footage and the existing Morabba brand typography. Share one story with the store link. Respect the already approved posting calendar; replace a planned item rather than duplicating it.
4. **10 minutes:** answer actual questions/reviews and remove friction in the download instructions.
5. **10 minutes:** review campaign website visits and store clicks. Choose tomorrow's task by installs and qualified clicks, not likes alone.

This plan prepares outreach copy; no messages, posts or store-listing edits were sent/published during the audit.

## Attribution and decision rules

- Use a different first-party landing link per partner, for example `https://adhkar.ir/?utm_source=p01&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=nour2k`. For Instagram use `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=nour2k`. The current analytics service recognizes these three UTM fields. Verify that visits and existing `store_click` events arrive before relying on the reporting.
- A website session, a store-button click and a completed install are distinct. UTM labels do not by themselves prove which individual installed from a store. Use the store's own attribution only where it is actually available; otherwise report campaign clicks and aggregate install changes separately.
- Use private install reports, not the public rounded badge, to decide whether a day had zero installs.
- After the first five placements, replace illustrative funnel rates with observed views and store clicks. If partners cannot supply views, mark reach unknown and compare qualified clicks instead.
- Do not declare a content experiment a winner on a handful of clicks. Prioritize directionally useful evidence, repeat the better hook, and keep uncertainty visible.
- If fewer than four relevant placements have been accepted by Day 5, or growth is less than 80% of the checkpoint, the 2K goal is at risk. Increase the amount of relevant partnership work; do not claim that more SEO articles will recover the gap within days.

### After deployment: exact Search Console follow-through

1. Check initial and rendered HTML for `/updates`, `/app`, `/adhkar/sobhgahi` and `/adhkar/shamgahi`. Confirm one matching canonical, truthful content, crawlable links and preserved download controls. Run `node scripts/verify-live-updates.mjs` and the article verifier.
2. Inspect the one soft-404 example. If the URL should exist, provide its real content/status; if retired, return a real 404/410 or redirect to a genuinely equivalent replacement. Do not redirect every missing URL to the homepage.
3. Inspect the two 404 examples; restore only accidentally missing destinations. A correctly retired URL is not a defect to eliminate.
4. Inspect all three redirected URLs. Permanent redirects from http/www or old equivalents can be intentional. Ensure the final destinations return 200, use one hop where practical, and that internal links/sitemaps use the final URLs. A 'failed validation' label alone does not justify removing a correct redirect.
5. Inspect the three crawled/not-indexed examples: selected canonical, actual content, internal linking and language alternates. There is no automatic code fix for this label.
6. Confirm sitemap processing is successful and only canonical 200 URLs are listed. Submit only if absent/failed; do not repeatedly resubmit unchanged files.
7. Request indexing once for the four changed priority URLs after live verification; start validation only for an exclusion actually fixed. Google says recrawling can take days to weeks and indexing is not guaranteed.
8. Review the same page/query cohorts on Days 7, 14 and 30, noting when Google last crawled the changed version. Compare complete equal windows and annotate the deployment date; do not attribute every traffic change to the edits.
